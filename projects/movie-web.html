Context

This project was a duo project where we had to create a movie, working together to show our understanding of parts of HTML.

We ended up creating something that was a mix of comedy and action where one person was taken and the two friends of the same person decided upon rescuing them.

Content

To create this project, we split up what we had to do and made up a plot that the project would revolve around. In the end, we settled on two main characters who are in debt and had to rescue a friend that they believed was kidnapped and taken away. We used questions in order to convey a mysterious feeling so that people would be curious on what happens next. The movie is supposed to be comedic and suspenseful at the right moments.

Reflection

Throughout this project, me and my partner were confused on mostly the part where we had to add the gradiant. At first, I thought we coded it incorrectly because it started from a lighter color into a darker color before immediately jumping back to the lighter color once again and so on. Once we searched up the problem online and attempted to add more text, we figured out that we just needed to add more things to the project so that it would look like how it is meant to look. This way, I learned that it is better if I waited until the entire project is done until I add a background gradiant.

Also, I learned that we still have to work with others in the future, despite the fact that being independent and not having to rely on anyone else is a important skill to have.

Anyhow, I feel as if me and my partner did an okay job in this project- we could have added a ending or a cliffhanger, though the questions were something that the viewer may have considered to be hooked onto the story. Overall, it was pretty awkward considering the fact that we never interacted until then.
